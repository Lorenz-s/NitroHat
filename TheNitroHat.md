**What is it?**
![NitroHat-Hardware](https://github.com/Lorenz-s/NitroHat/raw/main/images/Nitro_Hat_Detail.jpg)

The Nitro Hat is a **hi-hat controller** specifically designed for the Alesis Nitro, Nitro Max and Nitro Pro electronic drumkits.
It is intented to replace the DMhat Open/Close pedal provided with the drumkit:

![AlesisDMhat](https://github.com/Lorenz-s/NitroHat/raw/main/images/dmhat-1.jpg)

![AlesisDMhat2](https://github.com/Lorenz-s/NitroHat/raw/main/images/dmhat-2.jpg)

On the Nitro Pro, the pedal looks differently, but inside it's exactly the same, only a jack adaptor is needed:

![AlesisDMhatPro](https://github.com/Lorenz-s/NitroHat/raw/main/images/DMhat-NitroPro.jpg)

![AlesisDMhatPro2](https://github.com/Lorenz-s/NitroHat/raw/main/images/DMhat-NitroPro2.jpg)


**Why?**
Because the original pedal is in no way shape of form the same of a standard hi-hat, and it require very difficult movements or high forces to get the right sound out of the hi-hat.
Playing with the original pedal is not like playing with a real drumnkit, and I really wanted to have the feeling of a real hi-hat with my Alesis edrum.

**How does it work?**
It's actually very simple, the original pedal is changing resistance valies based on how you press on it.
I just designed the hardware to change resistance based on the movement of a real hi-hat stand!
While it took a lot of time to fine tune it, the end result is a simple but effective hi-hat controller that gets mounted directly on a standard hi-hat stand.

## **Key Features**

### **1. Standard Hi-Hat Pedal Compatible**
- Designed to install directly onto a stadard hi-hat stand.
- Works with clutch systems that use M10 and M12 bolts.

### **2. Natural Playability**
- No more over-pressing the pedal!
- Mimics the feel of an **acoustic hi-hat** for a more natural playing experience.

### **3. Full Expression**
- Play **Closed, Half-Open, Open, and Chick** sounds based on how you press the pedal.
- Finally, achieve the hi-hat control that you always wanted.

### **4. Plug-and-Play**
- Built **specifically for Alesis Nitro/Nitro Max/Nitro Pro**—no complicated setup.
- Just connect and start playing.
